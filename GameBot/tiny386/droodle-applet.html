About Droodle Applet

Droodle Applet — a reconstruction of the drawing tool Berkeley Systems ran alongside Get the Picture in 1999.

Droodles are saved as baseline 4:4:4 JPEG, the only flavour the game's decoder accepts. Your browser's own JPEG writer uses 4:2:0 and the game rejects it silently, so this page encodes the file itself.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Saving writes a file to your machine.

Rules & Authorization Agreement

PUT YOUR PICTURE IN THE GAME!

Draw and Submit a Droodle using the Droodle Applet. Play the game and see if your Droodle is used. You'll know it's yours because we'll put your name in the credit under the Droodle. The more you draw and submit, the greater the chance you have of getting your art and name into the game!!!

Rules

To be placed in the game, a submitted Droodle must not contain images that are deemed by the editorial staff to be profane, sexually suggestive or sexually explicit.

A Droodle must be submitted with the artist's real name or player alias and verifiable email address. Any submission by an artist whose player name contains questionable or foul language will not be put in the game.
